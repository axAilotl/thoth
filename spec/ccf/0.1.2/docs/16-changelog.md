# 16. 0.1.2 change log

0.1.2 is a narrow implementation-informed correction to 0.1.1. The
Record/Link/Blob algebra and canonical/projection boundary are unchanged.

- Origin idempotency includes `object_kind` and defines same-kind native suffixes.
- Admission authority classes are catalog-pinned to a deterministic evaluator.
- Foreign merge preserves unavailable compartment state and proof material.
- Valid producer batches retain chain continuity after content rejection;
  missing predecessors remain retryable.
- Suppression authority is canonical and journal-covered; lookups are projections.
- Suppression origin/content/scope derivations are separately registry-pinned.
- Mindpack custody is first-class and unsigned manifest claims are independently
  reconstructed and compared before mutation.
- Verification proves signed membership and admission correspondence.
- Catalog, genesis, mindpack, vectors, SQL, inventory, and checksums are regenerated.
- `docs/13-conformance.md` §13.8 adds implementation-derived and manifest-tamper
  conformance regressions.
